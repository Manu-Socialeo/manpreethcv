## 📖 Executive Summary & Impact
Built an adventure tourism platform connecting nature enthusiasts with Karnataka's Western Ghats trails (Kudremukha, Kumara Parvatha, Tadiandamol) and verified eco-friendly Sahyadri homestays.

---

## 📄 1. Product Requirements Document (PRD)

### 1.1 Problem Statement & Market Opportunity
Trekkers often struggle with unreliable trail permits, hidden homestay conditions, and lack of verified packing/fitness guidelines.

### 1.2 Proposed Solution & Value Proposition
A curated trail portal providing altitude profiles, trail difficulty ratings, weather advisories, gear checklists, and direct booking with local homestays.

### 1.3 Target User Personas
- Weekend Adventure Trekkers: Tech professionals from Bangalore and Mysore seeking weekend getaways.
- Eco-Homestay Hosts: Rural hosts in Chikmagalur, Coorg, and Sakleshpur wanting direct guest bookings.
- Forest Trail Guides: Sharing verified safety rules and permit guidance.

### 1.4 Core Functional Features
- **Trail Difficulty Matrix**:  Distance, elevation gain, fitness prerequisites, and best seasons.
- **Verified Homestay Booking**:  Authentic local Malnad food, campfire facilities, and tents.
- **Interactive Packing & Gear Checklist for monsoons vs winter treks.**: Interactive Packing & Gear Checklist for monsoons vs winter treks.
- **Instant Booking Inquiry with local host coordinates.**: Instant Booking Inquiry with local host coordinates.

---

## 🛠️ 2. Technical Requirements Document (TRD)

### 2.1 System Architecture
Modern Next.js application styled with Tailwind CSS, utilizing static pre-rendering for trail guides to ensure maximum search engine visibility.

### 2.2 Data Schema & State Management
Trail metadata, elevation records, homestay specs, and booking inquiry logs.

### 2.3 API & Service Integration
Serverless booking dispatch with WhatsApp click-to-chat integration.

### 2.4 Security, Performance & Reliability Standards
Sub-second load times on mobile devices even on spotty 3G/4G network zones in rural Ghat regions.
