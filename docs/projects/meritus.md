## 📖 Executive Summary & Impact
Developed a patient-centric, high-accessibility web platform for Meritus Diagnostics in Vijayanagar, Mysore, streamlining test discovery, preventive checkup packages, and home sample collection.

---

## 📄 1. Product Requirements Document (PRD)

### 1.1 Problem Statement & Market Opportunity
Patients frequently face anxiety and friction understanding blood test requirements, fasting protocols, and pricing when looking up local diagnostic labs.

### 1.2 Proposed Solution & Value Proposition
A clean, reassuring digital experience with categorized test directories, transparent test preparation instructions, and a one-click home collection booking funnel.

### 1.3 Target User Personas
- Patients & Families: Looking for reliable diagnostic tests, clear preparation guidelines (fasting vs non-fasting), and quick booking.
- Elderly & Mobility-Impaired Patients: Requiring seamless home blood collection scheduling.
- Doctors & Specialists: Recommending standardized health checkup packages to patients.

### 1.4 Core Functional Features
- **Comprehensive Test Catalog**:  Complete with preparation notes, sample types, and turnaround times.
- **Preventive Health Packages**:  Multi-tier executive, senior citizen, and women's health packages.
- **Home Sample Collection Request Form with automated WhatsApp routing.**: Home Sample Collection Request Form with automated WhatsApp routing.
- **Clinic Location, NABL compliance badges, and operating hours.**: Clinic Location, NABL compliance badges, and operating hours.

## 🛠️ 2. Technical Requirements Document (TRD)

### 2.1 System Architecture
Next.js with semantic HTML5 ensuring WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards for elderly and vision-impaired patients.

### 2.2 Data Schema & State Management
Relational test database with categorization, preparation guidelines, and pricing tiers.

### 2.3 API & Service Integration
Appointment submission handlers integrated with instant notification triggers.

### 2.4 Security, Performance & Reliability Standards
Strict patient data privacy adherence, HTTPS-only transport, zero trackers, and sub-second page loads.
