## 📖 Executive Summary & Impact
Crafted a luxury travel and wellness retreat booking platform for Shiva Retreats in Goa, empowering international practitioners from Europe, Americas, and Asia to discover authentic Yoga Teacher Training courses.

---

## 📄 1. Product Requirements Document (PRD)

### 1.1 Problem Statement & Market Opportunity
High-ticket spiritual retreats require immense visual trust, verified teacher lineage, and clear room/package options to convert international travelers booking months in advance.

### 1.2 Proposed Solution & Value Proposition
A visual-first web experience with immersive photography, comprehensive daily retreat schedules, accommodation breakdowns, and direct booking inquiries.

### 1.3 Target User Personas
- International Yoga Students: Traveling to India for certified 200hr/300hr Yoga Alliance courses.
- Wellness Seekers: Looking for rejuvenating beach retreats, Ayurvedic meals, and meditation.
- Retreat Directors: Managing seasonal cohort cohorts and room availability.

### 1.4 Core Functional Features
- **Curriculum & Schedule Explorer**:  Hour-by-hour breakdown of asana, pranayama, and philosophy classes.
- **Accommodation Showcase**:  Room tours (Cottages, Sea-View suites) with amenity listings.
- **Multi-Currency Pricing & Early Bird Discount Calculator.**: Multi-Currency Pricing & Early Bird Discount Calculator.
- **Direct WhatsApp & Email Enrollment Concierge.**: Direct WhatsApp & Email Enrollment Concierge.

---

## 🛠️ 2. Technical Requirements Document (TRD)

### 2.1 System Architecture
Next.js deployed across Vercel's global edge network to deliver instantaneous load speeds for users browsing from Europe and the US.

### 2.2 Data Schema & State Management
Retreat cohort schedule schema, room inventories, and inquiry records.

### 2.3 API & Service Integration
Serverless booking inquiry webhooks with automated confirmation autoresponders.

### 2.4 Security, Performance & Reliability Standards
Next-gen WebP image optimization, lazy loading, and strict SSL.
