## 📖 Executive Summary & Impact
Engineered a high-converting web platform for VO2 Max, a premier fitness and functional training studio in Mysore. The project drove substantial growth in local foot traffic and membership consultations.

---

## 📄 1. Product Requirements Document (PRD)

### 1.1 Problem Statement & Market Opportunity
Local fitness centers often lose leads due to slow, unoptimized landing pages that fail to appear on Google Local Search or effectively convey training methodology.

### 1.2 Proposed Solution & Value Proposition
A mobile-first, high-contrast performance site equipped with Local Business Schema markup, direct WhatsApp consultation funnels, and real member testimonials.

### 1.3 Target User Personas
- Fitness Enthusiasts & Athletes: Looking for specialized strength, conditioning, and VO2 max endurance training.
- Local Mysore Residents: Seeking verified workout schedules, trainer certifications, and quick membership inquiry.

### 1.4 Core Functional Features
- **High-Conversion Hero Section with one-tap WhatsApp / Call consultation triggers.**: High-Conversion Hero Section with one-tap WhatsApp / Call consultation triggers.
- **Interactive Training Modules**:  Breakdown of HIIT, Strength, Conditioning, and Recovery protocols.
- **Local SEO Schema Integration for Mysore geo-targeting.**: Local SEO Schema Integration for Mysore geo-targeting.
- **Frictionless Lead Capture modal.**: Frictionless Lead Capture modal.

## 🛠️ 2. Technical Requirements Document (TRD)

### 2.1 System Architecture
Static site generation with incremental static regeneration on Vercel, maximizing uptime and sub-second Time To First Byte (TTFB).

### 2.2 Data Schema & State Management
Lead inquiries captured and dispatched via serverless functions with email notifications.

### 2.3 API & Service Integration
Lightweight contact and appointment dispatch handlers.

### 2.4 Security, Performance & Reliability Standards
100% Core Web Vitals score, zero bloat, compressed WebP imagery, and responsive SVG icons.

## 🎨 3. UI/UX Design System & Experience

### 3.1 Design System & Aesthetic Direction
High-energy athletic aesthetic combining dark mode backdrop with neon energetic accents.
